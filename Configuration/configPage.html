Arcfin Sync

Defaults, locks and devices for everyone who uses Arcfin on this server.

How it works

  • Arcfin keeps each user's settings in step across their iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.
  • On Settings, give any setting a default (used until someone picks their own) or lock it (everyone gets your value and can't change it).
  • On Users & Devices, see every device signed in with Arcfin, rename or forget one, and give a single user their own defaults and locks.
  • Changes reach each device the next time Arcfin syncs: when it opens, comes to the front, or a setting changes.
